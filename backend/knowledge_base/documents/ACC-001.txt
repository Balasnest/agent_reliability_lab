ShopNova is committed to protecting the security of customer accounts and the personal information stored within them. Customers are encouraged to use a strong, unique password for their ShopNova account and to take advantage of the two-factor authentication option, which requires a secondary verification step when signing in from an unrecognized device or browser. Two-factor authentication is available in account security settings and provides meaningful additional protection against unauthorized access.

Password resets are handled exclusively through the email address associated with the customer's ShopNova account. Customers who need to reset their password should use the password reset option on the login page, which sends a time-limited secure reset link to the registered email. ShopNova customer support agents do not have the ability to reset passwords directly, view or retrieve existing passwords, or grant account access through any channel that bypasses the email-based verification process. If a customer no longer has access to the email address registered to their account, identity verification through an alternative process is required, which takes between 24 and 48 hours to complete.

Customers should never share their ShopNova account credentials with any third party, including individuals who identify themselves as ShopNova support representatives. ShopNova employees will never ask a customer to provide their password under any circumstances. If a customer receives a communication requesting their password or sensitive login details from someone claiming to represent ShopNova, they should treat it as a potential phishing attempt and report it to support immediately without providing any credentials.

Customers who suspect that their account has been accessed without authorization should contact ShopNova support as quickly as possible. A support agent can place a temporary lock on the account to prevent further unauthorized activity while an investigation is conducted. Any orders placed through a compromised account without the customer's knowledge are reviewed by the ShopNova security team and handled on a case-by-case basis.
