Many products available through ShopNova are accompanied by a manufacturer's warranty that provides coverage against defects in materials or workmanship for a defined period following the date of purchase. The specific terms, duration, and scope of coverage for any given product's warranty are set entirely by the manufacturer and vary considerably across brands and product types.

ShopNova does not administer, process, or fulfill manufacturer warranty claims. Warranty service is a direct relationship between the customer and the manufacturer of the product, and ShopNova plays no role in evaluating warranty eligibility, arranging repairs, facilitating replacements under warranty, or mediating warranty disputes. Customers who need to pursue a warranty claim must contact the manufacturer directly using the support contact information found in the product documentation, on the warranty card included with the item, or on the manufacturer's official website.

ShopNova can assist customers in the warranty process in a limited but important way: by providing documentation of the purchase. Most manufacturers require proof of purchase when a warranty claim is submitted, and ShopNova can issue a copy of the order confirmation or a purchase receipt upon request. Customers who need this documentation should contact ShopNova support and it will be provided promptly.

Customers should be careful not to confuse ShopNova's own defective item return policy with a manufacturer warranty. These are two separate options that may both be available depending on when the issue arises. ShopNova's defective item policy allows returns and refunds within 90 days of delivery for items that arrive defective or develop a defect within that period. Once the 90-day defective item window has closed, the manufacturer warranty, if applicable, becomes the appropriate path for addressing ongoing product quality issues. ShopNova support agents are not in a position to advise on the specific terms of any manufacturer's warranty, as those terms are entirely outside ShopNova's control and knowledge.
