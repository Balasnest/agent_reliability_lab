ShopNova maintains a dedicated policy for items that arrive damaged, fail to function upon first use, or develop a defect within a reasonable period after delivery. This policy is intended to provide a clear and consistent resolution path when a product does not meet basic quality and functionality expectations, and it applies across all product categories available on the platform.

Any item confirmed to be defective is eligible for return within 90 days of the confirmed delivery date, regardless of the product category or the standard return window that would otherwise apply to that category. A defective electronics item, for example, remains eligible for return under this policy even after the standard 15-day electronics return window has closed, provided the defect is identified and reported within the 90-day defective item window. Similarly, any other product category with a shorter or different return window defers to the 90-day defective item window when a defect is the basis for the return.

ShopNova provides a prepaid return shipping label for all defective item returns at no cost to the customer. This applies regardless of the customer's ShopNova Rewards tier and overrides the return shipping terms that would otherwise apply under the standard or electronics return policies. The free return label is provided at the time the return is approved.

Once the defective item is received and the defect is confirmed by the returns team, a full refund will be processed. Customers may be asked to provide a clear description of the defect and, where practical, photographs or a short description of the malfunction to support the return request. This documentation helps the returns team process the claim efficiently and allows ShopNova to flag quality concerns to the appropriate supplier.

Defects reported beyond the 90-day window fall outside the scope of this policy. In those situations, customers should contact the product manufacturer directly, as the manufacturer's warranty may still apply depending on the product and the nature of the defect. ShopNova does not process manufacturer warranty claims but can provide proof of purchase documentation to assist customers in that process.
