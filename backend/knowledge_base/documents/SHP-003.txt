On occasion, shipments may experience significant delays, become lost in transit, or arrive with visible damage to the contents. ShopNova has a defined process for investigating and resolving these situations and takes responsibility for ensuring customers receive their orders in acceptable condition.

If a customer believes their order has been lost, they should first allow seven full days beyond the expected delivery date before contacting ShopNova to file a lost shipment claim. Carrier networks sometimes experience delays that result in late deliveries without any visible tracking update, and the seven-day waiting period is designed to account for these cases before escalating to a formal investigation. Once seven days have passed without delivery, the customer should reach out to ShopNova support to open a carrier trace. ShopNova will submit the investigation request to the carrier and work toward a resolution within five to seven business days of the claim being filed.

If the carrier confirms the package was lost, ShopNova will offer the customer a choice between a full reshipment of the original order or a full refund to the original payment method. Refund processing timelines follow the standard schedule outlined in the billing policy.

If a package arrives with visible external damage, customers are encouraged to photograph the packaging before opening it if that is feasible. Any items found to be damaged upon delivery should be reported to ShopNova support within 48 hours of the delivery timestamp. Delivery damage is treated under the defective item policy, which covers a free prepaid return label and a full refund or replacement at the customer's discretion. Reports submitted more than 48 hours after delivery may still be considered, but prompt reporting is important for the carrier claim process and significantly improves the likelihood of a straightforward resolution.
