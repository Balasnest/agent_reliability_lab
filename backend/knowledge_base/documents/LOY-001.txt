ShopNova Rewards is the platform's tiered loyalty program, designed to provide increasing benefits to customers as they make more purchases. Membership in the program is automatic for all ShopNova account holders, and every dollar spent on eligible purchases earns one rewards point. Points accumulate over time and determine which of four membership tiers a customer belongs to: Standard, Silver, Gold, or Platinum.

Standard membership is the default tier assigned to all accounts. Standard members earn points on purchases but do not receive complimentary return shipping on non-defective returns. When a Standard member initiates a non-defective return, the cost of arranging and paying for return shipping is the customer's responsibility. Silver membership is unlocked at five hundred cumulative points. Silver members receive one complimentary prepaid return label per calendar year, which can be applied to any single non-defective return. Once the one free return label has been used within a given calendar year, any additional non-defective returns that year require the customer to cover the return shipping cost independently. Gold membership is reached at one thousand five hundred cumulative points and provides unlimited complimentary return shipping on all non-defective returns throughout the year, with no cap on the number of returns. Platinum membership, the highest tier, is achieved at five thousand cumulative points and includes all Gold benefits plus placement in a priority customer support queue. Platinum members who contact support are connected to an available agent more quickly than customers in lower tiers.

The return shipping benefits described above apply only to non-defective returns. When a customer is returning a defective item, a complimentary prepaid return label is always provided regardless of the customer's loyalty tier, as this is governed by the defective item policy rather than the Rewards Program.

Points are earned only on eligible product purchases and do not include taxes, shipping fees, or gift card purchases. Points are non-transferable and tied to the individual account. Customers can view their current point balance and tier status at any time through their ShopNova account dashboard, and tier upgrades take effect immediately upon crossing the relevant threshold.
