ShopNova offers standard shipping on all eligible orders placed through the platform. Under normal operating conditions, standard shipping delivers within three to five business days from the date the order ships. Before shipment occurs, orders go through a processing period of one to two business days during which the order is confirmed, picked, and prepared for dispatch. Customers should therefore plan for a total of approximately five to seven business days between the date of purchase and the expected delivery date when choosing standard shipping.

Standard shipping is provided at no charge for orders with an item subtotal of thirty-five dollars or more before taxes and fees. Orders with a subtotal below thirty-five dollars are subject to a flat shipping fee of four dollars and ninety-nine cents. The thirty-five dollar threshold is applied to the item subtotal only and does not include taxes, any handling fees, or optional shipping upgrades the customer may select.

Delivery estimates are given in business days and do not include weekends or federal holidays. During high-volume periods such as major promotional events or the winter holiday season, processing and delivery timelines may be longer than usual. ShopNova communicates anticipated delays at checkout and in order confirmation emails when relevant.

Once an order has shipped, customers receive a shipping confirmation email that includes a tracking number. This number can be used on the carrier's website to follow the shipment's progress. ShopNova works with multiple carriers, and the specific carrier assigned to an order depends on the delivery address, package size, and product type.

Standard shipping is available for most items shipped to addresses within the contiguous United States. Some oversized products, restricted items, or deliveries to certain remote locations may require modified shipping arrangements, and any such limitations will be disclosed at the time of checkout before the order is finalized.
