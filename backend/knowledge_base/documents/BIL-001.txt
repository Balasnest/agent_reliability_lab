When a return is approved and the returned item has been received and inspected at ShopNova's fulfillment center, a refund is initiated promptly. The time it takes for the refund to appear in the customer's account depends on the payment method used at the time of the original purchase.

Refunds to credit cards are typically completed within three to five business days from the date ShopNova initiates the refund. Refunds to debit cards take somewhat longer and generally appear within five to seven business days, as bank account processing involves additional steps that extend the timeline. Customers who paid via PayPal can expect their refund to process within three to five business days, though PayPal's own internal processing may affect when funds become accessible within the PayPal account. Refunds issued as ShopNova store credit are the fastest option and are applied to the customer's account within one to two business days, after which the credit is immediately available for use on future purchases.

ShopNova initiates refunds as soon as the return inspection is complete, but the point at which the funds appear in the customer's account is controlled by the customer's financial institution or payment processor, not by ShopNova. If the stated timeline has passed and the refund has not appeared, customers should first check with their bank or payment provider, as delays on the financial institution side are the most common cause. If the bank confirms no refund is pending, customers should then contact ShopNova support for further investigation.

When a return involves only some items from a multi-item order, the refund amount reflects the cost of the returned items plus any applicable taxes on those items. Original shipping fees paid at the time of purchase are not refunded unless the return was caused by a fulfillment error on ShopNova's part or the item is confirmed as defective.
