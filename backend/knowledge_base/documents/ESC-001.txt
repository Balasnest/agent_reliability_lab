ShopNova customer support agents are responsible for identifying situations that require escalation beyond what can be resolved at the standard agent level. Escalation means creating a formal support ticket that is routed to a senior specialist or the appropriate internal team. When a case is escalated, the customer must be informed that their issue has been elevated and must be given an estimated timeframe for a follow-up response. Agents should not resolve or close an interaction in which an escalation trigger has been identified without first creating the ticket.

There are four conditions that each independently require immediate escalation. The first is order value: any support interaction that involves an order with a total value exceeding five hundred dollars must be escalated, regardless of the nature of the issue being discussed. High-value transactions carry elevated financial and reputational risk and require review by senior staff before any resolution commitment is made on ShopNova's behalf. The second trigger is repeated contact: if a customer has reached out to ShopNova support three or more times regarding the same unresolved issue within the past thirty days, the case must be escalated. Repeated contact on the same issue indicates that standard resolution processes have not been sufficient, and senior intervention is required. The third trigger is any mention of legal, regulatory, or external dispute action: if a customer references fraud, legal proceedings, involvement of a regulatory body such as the Better Business Bureau or the Federal Trade Commission, or any indication that they have initiated or plan to initiate a formal external dispute process, the interaction must be escalated immediately. The escalation ticket in these cases must include verbatim or closely paraphrased notes of what the customer stated, and the ticket should be assigned the highest available priority level. The fourth trigger is an explicit customer request: if a customer asks to speak with a supervisor, manager, escalation team, or anyone other than the current support agent, that request must be fulfilled by initiating an escalation. Agents should not attempt to persuade the customer that escalation is unnecessary or that the current agent can handle the matter.

When creating an escalation ticket, the agent must include the customer's account identifier, the relevant order number, a clear summary of the issue and the conversation to date, and the specific trigger or triggers that prompted the escalation. The priority level assigned to the ticket should reflect the urgency and sensitivity of the situation.
