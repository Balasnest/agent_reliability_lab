For customers who need their order to arrive faster than the standard three-to-five business day window, ShopNova offers two expedited shipping options at checkout. Two-day shipping delivers within two business days of the shipment date and is available for a flat fee of nine dollars and ninety-nine cents per order. Overnight shipping guarantees delivery on the next business day after shipment and is available for nineteen dollars and ninety-nine cents per order. Both options are presented at checkout for eligible orders shipping to eligible destinations.

To qualify for two-day or overnight delivery on a given day, the order must be placed and confirmed before two o'clock in the afternoon Eastern Standard Time. Orders placed after this daily cutoff, or on weekends and federal holidays, will begin processing on the next business day. This means an overnight order placed at three in the afternoon on a Friday will not ship until Monday and will deliver on Tuesday, not Saturday. Customers with time-sensitive delivery needs should confirm the cutoff time requirements before completing their purchase.

Not all products and destinations are eligible for expedited shipping. Items that are oversized, items fulfilled by third-party Marketplace sellers, and deliveries to P.O. boxes or select remote addresses may not qualify for two-day or overnight fulfillment. Availability of each shipping option will be shown or hidden automatically at checkout based on the items in the cart and the delivery address entered.

Expedited shipping fees are charged per order and are non-refundable when a return is initiated due to a change of mind. If ShopNova or the assigned carrier fails to meet the guaranteed delivery window due to a carrier error that is within the carrier's control, customers may request a review for a possible refund of the shipping fee. Delays attributable to weather events, natural disasters, or other circumstances beyond the carrier's control are not eligible for shipping fee refunds.
