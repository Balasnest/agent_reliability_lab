ShopNova accepts returns on most items within 30 days of the confirmed delivery date. To be eligible for a return, items must be in their original condition, unused, and in their original packaging with all included accessories and documentation present. Returns initiated after the 30-day window will not be accepted under standard terms, though exceptions exist for items that are confirmed to be defective, which are handled under a separate policy.

Once a returned item is received and inspected at our fulfillment center, a refund is issued to the original payment method used at the time of purchase. Customers should allow the standard processing time for the refund to appear, as this varies by payment type and is described in detail in the billing and refunds policy.

Customers should be aware that certain product categories operate under different return conditions. Electronics in particular are subject to a more specific return policy due to the nature of those products, and ShopNova strongly recommends reviewing the electronics-specific guidelines before initiating any return involving a device or electronic accessory. The general 30-day window described here does not override category-specific policies where those policies apply.

Return shipping eligibility is not universal and depends on a combination of the customer's ShopNova Rewards membership tier and the reason for the return. Customers returning a defective item are always eligible for a prepaid return label at no cost. For non-defective returns, whether the customer receives a prepaid label or must arrange and pay for their own return shipping is determined by the ShopNova Rewards Program terms applicable to their account tier.

Items sold by third-party sellers through the ShopNova Marketplace are not governed by this standard return policy. Those purchases fall under the Marketplace Return Policy, which differs in several important ways including the available refund method and responsibility for return shipping costs. Customers can confirm whether their item was sold by ShopNova directly or by a Marketplace seller by checking their order confirmation.

To begin a return, customers should contact ShopNova customer support, where an agent will verify the order details and guide the customer through the process appropriate to their situation.
