Items listed and sold by independent third-party sellers through the ShopNova Marketplace are governed by a return policy that differs in important ways from the standard ShopNova return terms. Customers purchasing from Marketplace sellers should familiarize themselves with these terms before completing a purchase, as the conditions and outcomes of a return are meaningfully different from those that apply to items sold directly by ShopNova.

Marketplace items are eligible for return within 30 days of the confirmed delivery date. Unlike electronics sold directly by ShopNova, which carry a 15-day return window, all Marketplace items regardless of product category are eligible under the standard 30-day window. Items must be returned in their original condition with all packaging, accessories, and documentation intact.

Refunds for all Marketplace returns are issued exclusively as ShopNova store credit. This applies universally to all Marketplace transactions and is not modified by the customer's loyalty tier, account standing, original payment method, or any other factor. Customers who expect a refund to their credit card, debit card, or other original payment method should be aware that this is not available for purchases made from Marketplace sellers. The store credit issued is applied to the customer's ShopNova account and can be used toward any future purchase on the platform.

Return shipping for Marketplace items is the customer's responsibility. The buyer must arrange and cover the cost of shipping the item back to the seller. The single exception to this rule is when the item arrived in a defective condition or was materially different from how it was described in the product listing. In those cases, ShopNova will provide a prepaid return label to the customer. However, even in defective Marketplace returns where a prepaid label is provided, the refund is still issued as store credit rather than to the original payment method.

For unresolved disputes with Marketplace sellers, including non-delivery, significant product mislisting, or seller non-responsiveness, customers should contact ShopNova support, which mediates between buyers and Marketplace sellers and can intervene when a seller is not meeting their obligations.
