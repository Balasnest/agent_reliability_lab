All electronic products sold directly by ShopNova are subject to a 15-day return window measured from the confirmed delivery date. This category includes televisions, laptops, desktop computers, smartphones, tablets, cameras, audio equipment, gaming consoles, and related accessories and peripherals. The 15-day window supersedes the standard 30-day return period that applies to other product categories and is not extended by loyalty tier status or account history.

To qualify for a return under this policy, an electronic item must meet one of two conditions: it must be in its original factory-sealed, unopened condition, or it must be confirmed as defective. Electronic items that have been opened and are functioning as intended are not eligible for return once the 15-day window has closed. Customers who are uncertain about a purchase are strongly encouraged to begin the return process promptly rather than waiting, as no exceptions to the 15-day limit are granted for change-of-mind returns on opened, functioning electronics.

When a return is approved, the refund is issued to the original payment method on file and follows the standard refund processing timeline. For defective electronics, ShopNova provides a prepaid return shipping label at no additional cost. For non-defective electronics being returned within the 15-day window, the cost of return shipping is determined by the customer's ShopNova Rewards tier in the same manner as other product categories.

Manufacturer warranties are entirely separate from ShopNova's return process and are not administered by ShopNova. If an electronic device develops a fault after the 15-day return window has passed but the item is not defective in the sense covered by ShopNova's defective item policy, customers should contact the product's manufacturer directly to pursue a warranty claim. ShopNova can provide purchase receipts as proof of purchase upon request to assist with the manufacturer warranty process.

Customers who believe a return request has been incorrectly denied should contact support to request a review of the order details and applicable policy.
