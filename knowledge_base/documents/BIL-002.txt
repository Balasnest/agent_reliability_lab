ShopNova strongly encourages customers who have a concern about a charge on their account to contact ShopNova customer support directly before initiating a dispute or chargeback through their bank or credit card provider. ShopNova support agents are able to investigate billing discrepancies, reverse charges that are found to be incorrect, issue refunds for valid claims, and escalate complex cases to the internal finance team. Reaching out to ShopNova directly is almost always faster than the formal chargeback process and avoids the complications that chargebacks introduce.

Customers should understand that filing a chargeback dispute with their bank or card issuer has immediate consequences for their ShopNova account. When a chargeback is initiated, ShopNova is notified by the payment processor and is required to place a hold on the customer's account pending the outcome of the dispute investigation. During this account freeze, the customer will be unable to place new orders, access their full order history, use ShopNova store credit, or interact with their account in most ways. The freeze is applied as a standard procedural requirement and is not a penalty judgment; it reflects the uncertainty of account status during an open dispute.

The resolution timeline for chargeback disputes is determined by the customer's financial institution and typically ranges from thirty to ninety days. If the investigation concludes in ShopNova's favor, the disputed amount may be returned to ShopNova and the original charge reinstated to the customer's account. If the investigation concludes in the customer's favor, the refund is processed through the bank channel and the account hold is lifted once the dispute is formally closed.

Customers with urgent billing concerns, including suspected unauthorized charges, are advised to contact ShopNova support immediately rather than going directly to the bank. In cases of suspected account compromise, support agents can take protective action on the account while the billing issue is simultaneously investigated.
